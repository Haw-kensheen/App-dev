# Favorite Movie
John Wick
Overview

John Wick is an action movie about a former hitman who tries to live a normal life, but after something terrible happens, he gets pulled back into the dangerous world he left behind. The movie is fast-paced, emotional, and full of impressive fight scenes.

What the Movie Is About

A retired assassin goes on a mission for revenge after losing something very important to him.

Why I Chose This Movie
Reasons

Really cool action scenes

Interesting world with rules and special hotels

Simple but emotional story

Great main character

My Favorite Parts

The “gun-fu” fighting style

The Continental Hotel

The gold coin system

The mysterious assassin rules

Favorite Quote

“Yeah… I’m thinking I’m back.”
